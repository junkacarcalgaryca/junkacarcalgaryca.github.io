What decides your cash offer

Every vehicle is priced on its own condition, so two cars of the same age can get very different numbers. When you ask for cash for cars Calgary buyers to quote you, these six things drive the figure.

Year, make and model

Popular trucks and SUVs hold value even when worn out, because parts and resale demand stay strong.

Runs or doesn't

A car that starts and drives is worth more than a dead one, but a non-runner still has real value.

Body and frame

Rust, collision, flood and Calgary hail all change the number. Be honest and the quote will hold.

Complete or stripped

Leave the wheels, battery and catalytic converter on. Pulling parts usually costs you more than they sell for.

Scrap metal prices

For end-of-life vehicles, the weight of recoverable steel, aluminum and other metals sets the floor.

Mileage

Lower kilometres help a running car. For scrap, they matter much less than weight and completeness.

Why old cars in Calgary hit the end of the road sooner

Calgary is tough on vehicles. Road salt and sand eat undercarriages from November to March. Chinook swings between mild and deep freeze crack seals and drain batteries. Hail season can write a car off in minutes, and daily runs on Deerfoot, Stoney and Crowchild add kilometres quickly.

When a repair quote passes half the car's value, or the same car keeps failing, selling it as is usually saves money. It also frees the driveway, parkade stall and insurance bill.
